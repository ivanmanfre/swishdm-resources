When deciding between Magic Spoon and Farm Girl for a healthier, low-carb, or keto-friendly cereal in the US, the right choice ultimately comes down to your dietary restrictions (specifically dairy/vegan), protein preferences, and availability.

Both brands target the nostalgic "childhood cereal" market with 0g of sugar, but they approach formulation very differently.

1\. Ingredients & Protein Source (The Biggest Difference)
  • Magic Spoon (Dairy-Based): Uses a milk protein blend (casein and whey). This provides a complete profile of essential amino acids and gives it a texture and nutrient profile closer to a protein shake. It does contain dairy, so it is not vegan.
  • Farm Girl (Plant-Based / Vegan): Uses a plant-protein blend primarily built from faba bean protein, potato protein, and sometimes oat protein. It is 100% vegan, dairy-free, and soy-free.
2\. Nutritional Comparison (Per Serving)
  • Magic Spoon: Typically runs around 130–140 calories, 13g to 14g of protein, ~4g to 5g net carbs, and roughly 6g of fat (using a high-oleic sunflower/avocado oil blend). It is very low in fiber (often 0g to 1g).
  • Farm Girl: Usually yields around 12g of protein, 0g of sugar, but often features significantly more dietary fiber (ranging from 6g to 10g depending on the flavor) and lower fat content.
3\. Sweeteners Used
  • Magic Spoon: Sweetened with a blend of allulose (a rare natural sugar that doesn't spike blood sugar) and monk fruit.
  • Farm Girl: Relies heavily on monk fruit juice concentrate and alternative fibers/sweeteners like tagatose or isomaltose.
4\. Availability & Cost in the US
  • Magic Spoon: Widely available across the US. You can buy it directly from their website, on Amazon, and in major mainstream grocery stores (like Target, Kroger, and Sprouts).
  • Farm Girl: Originating from Canada, Farm Girl has been expanding its footprint into the US (sometimes spotted at regional grocers or Costco in specific areas), but it is still primarily an online order or specialty health-market find for most US shoppers.
Which one should you buy?
  • Buy Magic Spoon if: You tolerate dairy well, want a higher-fat/lower-carb profile that fits strictly into a keto diet, prefer whey/casein protein, and want something easy to find at your local US grocery store or Target.
  • Buy Farm Girl if: You are vegan, dairy-free, or sensitive to whey/casein, want a plant-based protein source (like faba bean), and prefer a cereal that packs a higher punch of dietary fiber.
