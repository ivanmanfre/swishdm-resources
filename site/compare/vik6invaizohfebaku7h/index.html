Key Components & How They Work
  • LED Wheels (Dynamo-Powered): Most light-up longboard wheels (like Sunset Skateboards or Fireball Tinder LEDs) require no batteries or charging . They use a magnetic spacer inside the wheel core that spins around a copper coil as you roll, generating electricity through electromagnetic induction to light up internal LEDs.
  • Specs to look for: 65mm–70mm diameter and 78A–80A durometer (soft polyurethane) so they absorb cracks and rough pavement smoothly.
  • Deck Underglow (Battery/USB-Rechargeable): Boards with illuminated decks either use a translucent polycarbonate/acrylic deck (e. g., Sunset Flare) with an internal battery pack, or a standard maple/bamboo deck fitted with adhesive, weather-resistant RGB LED strips powered by a rechargeable lithium pack mounted underneath.
  • Trucks & Bearings: Standard 7-inch (180mm) reverse-kingpin trucks provide smooth carving, paired with ABEC-7 or ABEC-9 bearings to help overcome the slight magnetic drag created by dynamo wheel spacers.

How the Lighting Systems Work
  • Wheel Lights (Kinetic / Magnetic): Most LED longboard wheels require no batteries or charging . They use an internal magnetic spacer sandwiched between the two wheel bearings. As the wheel spins, electromagnetic induction powers the embedded LEDs—meaning the faster you roll, the brighter they glow.
  • Deck Lights (Rechargeable Underglow): Deck illumination usually comes from adhesive LED strips mounted along the underside perimeter or edge-lit acrylic decks. These run on a rechargeable lithium-ion battery pack mounted underneath the board and are typically controlled via a wireless remote or smartphone app.
