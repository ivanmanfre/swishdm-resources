Quilted golf mallet putter covers combine padded protection against bag chatter with a classic, textured aesthetic. Because mallet putters vary widely in footprint and shaft entry, choosing the right cover comes down to shape compatibility, closure type, and weather resistance.

Key Buying Factors
  • Putter Shape & Size: Standard "mid-mallet" covers fit compact shapes (like a TaylorMade Rossa or Odyssey #7), while "oversized" or "spider" covers are required for high- MOI ? mallets (like a TaylorMade Spider or Scotty Cameron Phantom X).
  • Hosel / Shaft Entry: Center-shafted mallets need a dedicated center-entry opening. Standard heel-shafted covers will not close properly over a center shaft.
  • Closure Mechanism:
  • Magnetic: Quiet, fast to remove, and won't wear out over time.
  • Velcro (Hook-and-Loop): Secure, though the scratchy side can snag knit sweaters or lose grip after a few seasons.
  • Outer Material & Lining: Look for water-resistant nylon, waxed canvas, or synthetic leather (PU) on the outside, paired with a plush fleece or faux-fur lining on the inside to protect delicate putter finishes.

What to Check Before Buying
  • Hosel / Shaft Placement: Mallet covers are cut differently depending on whether your putter is heel-shafted (most common), center-shafted , or has a single-bend/plumber's neck . Center-shafted mallets require a cover with a centered rear opening so the magnets close cleanly.
