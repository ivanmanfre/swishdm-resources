We built a free AI Buyer Comparison Report for ecommerce brands. And you can get one for your own store. When a buyer can't decide between you and a competitor, they can ask ChatGPT or Gemini to choose for them. So we tried it on two fire pit brands, Breeo and Solo Stove, and read 36 answers on 15 October. 16 of those answers, all from Gemini, said Breeo's X Series has a built-in sear plate rim, which Breeo's own store lists among discontinued models. Gemini didn't search the web once in its 18 answers, so everything it said came from what it had already learned. Your report shows the same for your brand: - Who gets picked for each buyer question, with each brand typed first - What the answers say about your prices, warranty, returns and products, checked against your own store - The outside pages the answers lean on, and how old they are - Three things worth fixing first It's one dated sample and the answers change from run to run, so every answer is in the report in full. Whether you run the store yourself or head up marketing, we hope you'll find it useful. We're giving the report away for free. Access is just below.
