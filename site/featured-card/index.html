Add it in about two minutes, from a computer

  1. Open your LinkedIn profile and click Add section under your name.
  2. Open Recommended, then Add featured.
  3. Click +, pick Add a link, paste the link and click Add.
  4. LinkedIn fills in its own title and picture. Upload the image above, then paste the title and description.
  5. Click Save and drag the card to the first spot in Featured.
